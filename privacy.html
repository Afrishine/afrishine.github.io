Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what Afrishine Hub collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your email or Google sign-in details, the name you choose, and a profile photo, bio and location if you add them.
  • What you share: posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, follows, Marketplace listings, reviews and reports, and Inbox messages.
  • Love Connection (adults only): your age and, if you choose, a WhatsApp number shown on your posts.
  • Verification: details you send when applying for a Verified badge.
  • Notifications: a device notification token if you allow push notifications.
  • On your device: small settings and cached files so the app loads faster.

How we use it

To run the service (sign-in, feeds, messages, Marketplace, notifications), keep it safe, review reports and verification requests, and improve it. We do not sell your personal data.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, notifications) — accounts and app data.
  • Cloudflare — storing and delivering photos, videos and files.
  • Vercel — hosting the website.

What other members can see

Your name, photo, bio, posts, Marketplace listings and Verified status are visible to other members, and public posts can be shared by link. Inbox messages are visible only to the people in the conversation and to Afrishine administrators when needed to handle abuse or a report.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep data while your account is active. You can delete your own posts and listings in the app. To ask for other data to be deleted, use the Contact page inside Afrishine Hub.

Children

Afrishine Hub is not directed to children, and Love Connection is for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: use the Contact page inside Afrishine Hub.
