Love Connection ❤️

A respectful space for adults aged 18 and above to connect and get to know one another.

Age Confirmation

Love Connection is only for adults aged 18 and above. Create an account or sign in, then complete your Love Profile to continue. This is an age declaration, not official identity verification.

❤️ Your Love Profile

Your Afrishine account is used to sign in. Enter your real age (18+) and choose the username people will see in Love Connection. WhatsApp is optional and will only be shown on your Love posts when you choose to share it.

✍️ Create a Love Connection Post

Example: “I am looking for a partner. I am 20 years old. I would like to meet someone respectful.”

Think carefully before sharing contact details publicly, and never send money to someone you only know online.

Account

Privacy and Security

  • We store the details you provide: username, bio, profile photo, posts, comments, messages, marketplace listings and the email linked to your account.
  • Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (email or Google) and, inside Pi Browser, by Pi Network. Afrishine Hub never sees your password.
  • Photos and videos you upload are stored on Afrishine Hub media storage.
  • Love Connection is for adults aged 18 and above. Your WhatsApp number is shown only if you choose to include it in a post.
  • Never share your PIN, password or verification codes, and never send money to someone you only know online.
  • You can report posts and block users in Love Connection, and report Marketplace listings.
